About

Sam Rudd

Contemporary landscape painter.

Contemporary landscape paintings, inspired by the British landscape, wild places and coastal life.

Sam paints the coast and the countryside — boats pulled up on the sand, a church across the water, the worn patchwork of fields on a hillside. The paintings sit somewhere between what was in front of her and what she remembers of it, built up in soft blocks of colour and left loose enough to breathe.

A fuller biography is on its way. In the meantime, the paintings themselves are the best introduction.
